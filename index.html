课程简介About the course

让人工智能成为科学发现的方法，而不只是分析工具 Make AI a method of scientific discovery, not merely an analysis tool

本课程面向人工智能驱动科学研究（AI for Science，AI4S）的前沿发展，是全校研究生与高年级本科生的关键科学智能通识课程。课程按“基础—领域—实践”三段式组织：第一部分为科学智能导论与基础技术，系统讲授科研范式变革、科学数据治理与 AI Ready、跨模态跨尺度科学数据统一表征、科学机器学习与科学基础模型、科学推理大模型与科学智能体等贯通各学科的共性技术；第二部分为典型领域 AI4S 实践与发展趋势，围绕 AI+通信、AI+数学、AI+安全、AI+医学、AI+光电等方向展示深度交叉的研究范式与前沿趋势；第三部分为科学智能实践与科学伦理，邀请书生等自主科学发现平台团队授课并组织学生上机探索实践，同时贯彻科研诚信与科学伦理教育。课程期间穿插大师讲堂（根据受邀专家灵活安排、不预设固定学时）。

This course addresses the frontier of Artificial Intelligence for Science (AI4S) and serves as a key general course on scientific intelligence for graduate and senior undergraduate students across all disciplines. It is organized in three parts. Part I, Introduction and Foundational Technologies, systematically covers the transformation of scientific paradigms and the cross-disciplinary common techniques of AI4S, including scientific data governance and AI-readiness, unified cross-modal and cross-scale representation of scientific data, scientific machine learning and foundation models for science, and scientific reasoning models and agents. Part II, Domain Practices and Trends, presents deep AI-discipline integration in communications, mathematics, security, medicine, and optoelectronics. Part III, Practice and Research Ethics, invites the teams of autonomous scientific-discovery platforms to teach and to guide hands-on exploratory practice, while embedding research integrity and ethics. Distinguished Scholar Lectures are interspersed and scheduled flexibly.

通过学习，学生能够建立系统的科学智能知识体系，掌握科学数据治理、统一表征、科学基础模型、科学推理与智能体等共性技术，理解 AI 由数据分析工具向科学建模、科学推理与自主发现主体演进的基本逻辑；能够结合本学科的科学对象、数据特征与机理知识分析 AI4S 问题，在真实科研平台上完成“数据—模型—推理—验证”闭环的探索实践，并树立严谨求证、负责任创新的科研价值观。

Upon completion, students will master the common enabling technologies of AI4S, understand the evolution of AI from a data-analysis tool toward an agent for scientific modeling, reasoning, and autonomous discovery, and complete a reproducible closed-loop practice on real research platforms.

课程目标Learning goals

知识、方法与科学责任同步建立 Knowledge, method, and scientific responsibility together

01

知识与技能

Knowledge and skills

掌握 AI4S 的基本概念与主要研究范式，重点掌握科学数据治理与 AI-Ready 数据集构建、跨模态跨尺度统一表征、科学机器学习与科学基础模型、科学推理大模型与科学智能体等共性技术；了解 AI4 通信、数学、安全、医学、光电等交叉方向的典型科学问题与前沿趋势。

Master the core concepts and paradigms of AI4S, especially data governance and AI-ready datasets, unified cross-modal representation, scientific machine learning and foundation models, and scientific reasoning models and agents; understand representative problems and trends across the course domains.

02

过程与方法

Process and methods

培养从科学问题出发分析和设计 AI 研究方案的能力，能够结合科学数据、领域知识与机理规律进行问题建模；能够在书生等真实科研平台上完成数据准备、建模计算与假设验证的闭环实践。

Learn to start from a scientific question, design an AI research plan, and complete a closed loop of data preparation, modeling, and hypothesis validation on real research platforms such as InternLM / ShuSheng.

03

情感态度与价值观

Values and responsibility

树立“AI 生成 ≠ 科学发现、模型预测 ≠ 科学事实、计算结果须经证据与实验检验”的科研意识，正确认识 AI 能力边界及其安全、伦理与社会责任。

Internalize that AI generation is not scientific discovery, model prediction is not scientific fact, and computed results must be tested by evidence and experiment — with clear awareness of safety, ethics, and social responsibility.

教学内容Curriculum

基础 — 领域 — 实践，三十二学时贯通 Foundations, domains, and practice across 32 hours

第一部分 8 学时，第二部分 20 学时，第三部分 4 学时。大师讲堂结合课程内容穿插安排，学时不预先固定。

Part I: 8 hours. Part II: 20 hours. Part III: 4 hours. Distinguished Scholar Lectures are scheduled flexibly and are not assigned a fixed hour budget in advance.

Part I · 8h

科学智能的原理与共性技术

Principles and common techniques

建立统一方法论，打通数据、表征、模型与智能体。

Build a shared methodology across data, representation, models, and agents.

1.1 科学智能导论：科研范式变革与 AI4S 总体框架

1.1 Introduction: paradigm shift and the AI4S framework

2h

AI4S 发展背景与国家级战略布局；科学发现范式演进（实验—理论—计算—数据—智能）；数据驱动、知识驱动与机理驱动的科学建模；“科学假设—计算推演—实验验证—反馈修正”闭环；AI4S 区别于通用 AI 应用的核心特征。

National strategies and the AI4S landscape; the evolution of discovery paradigms; data-, knowledge-, and mechanism-driven modeling; the hypothesis–computation–experiment–feedback loop; what distinguishes AI4S from generic AI applications.

1.2 AI Ready 科学数据

1.2 AI-ready scientific data

2h

科学数据的多源、异构、高维、稀缺与含噪特性；采集、清洗、标注、质量控制与标准化；FAIR 原则与科学数据基座；AI-Ready 数据集的构建与共享；偏差、可追溯性与可复现性。

Heterogeneous, high-dimensional, scarce, and noisy scientific data; collection, cleaning, annotation, and quality control; FAIR principles; building and sharing AI-ready datasets; bias, provenance, and reproducibility.

1.3 跨模态、跨尺度统一表征与科学基础模型

1.3 Unified representation and foundation models for science

2h

序列、图、图像、光谱与结构等科学数据表征；token 化与统一表征空间；PINN、神经算子与代理模型；科学基础模型的构建范式与迁移。

Representing sequences, graphs, images, spectra, and structures; tokenization and a unified latent space; PINNs, neural operators, and surrogate models; construction and transfer of scientific foundation models.

1.4 科学推理大模型与科学智能体

1.4 Scientific reasoning models and agents

2h

思维链推理、科学推理评测与推理专用大模型；规划、工具调用、记忆与多智能体协作；AI co-scientist 与自主科学发现；自主实验室与“干湿闭环”。

Chain-of-thought reasoning and science-specific evaluation; planning, tool use, memory, and multi-agent collaboration; AI co-scientists; autonomous labs and the dry–wet experimental loop.

Part II · 20h

典型学科的 AI4S 范式与前沿

Domain paradigms and frontiers

根据技术前沿，每学期开课模块可灵活调整。

Offered modules may shift each term with the research frontier.

2.1 AI4 通信：6G 智能 AI 原生通信

2.1 AI4 Communications: 6G AI-native systems

4h

6G AI-native 架构；物理层智能与端到端智能通信；网络规划与资源调度；信道大模型、网络数字孪生、通感算智一体化。

6G AI-native architecture; physical-layer intelligence and end-to-end learned communications; network planning and scheduling; channel foundation models, digital twins, and integrated sensing–communication–computing.

2.2 AI4 数学：科学机器学习与可验证数学推理

2.2 AI4 Mathematics: scientific ML and verifiable reasoning

4h

PINN、DeepONet 与傅里叶神经算子；正/逆问题与不确定性量化；自动定理证明、Lean 与神经符号推理。

PINNs, DeepONet, and Fourier neural operators; forward/inverse problems and uncertainty quantification; automated theorem proving, Lean, and neuro-symbolic reasoning.

2.3 AI4 安全：智能安全分析与可信协议验证

2.3 AI4 Security: intelligent analysis and trustworthy verification

4h

异常检测与安全缺陷发现；大语言模型分析安全协议的能力与局限；形式化建模与自动验证；AI 与形式化方法的结合。

Anomaly detection and vulnerability discovery; capabilities and limits of LLMs on security protocols; formal modeling and automated verification; combining AI with formal methods.

2.4 AI4 医学：多尺度生命健康建模与智能科学发现

2.4 AI4 Medicine: multi-scale health modeling and discovery

4h

多模态医学基础模型；疾病进程与治疗响应建模；单细胞与多组学；蛋白质结构预测与生成式药物设计。

Multimodal medical foundation models; disease progression and treatment-response modeling; single-cell and multi-omics; protein structure prediction and generative molecular design.

2.5 AI4 材料：材料与纳电子器件智能设计

2.5 AI4 Materials: intelligent materials and nanoelectronics design

4h

结构表征、结构—性质预测与机器学习势函数；纳电子器件正向建模与逆向设计。

Structure characterization, structure–property prediction, and machine-learning potentials; forward modeling and inverse design of nanoelectronic devices.

2.6 AI4 芯片：人工智能驱动的集成电路与 EDA

2.6 AI4 Chips: AI-driven IC and EDA design

4h

集成电路及 EDA 流程；大模型与智能体驱动的数字芯片前端设计；后端智能优化；产业落地与趋势。

IC and EDA workflows; LLM- and agent-driven digital front-end design; AI-assisted back-end optimization; industrial adoption and outlook.

第二部分合计 20 学时，六个方向每学期不必全部开设，开课模块随前沿发展调整。

Part II totals 20 hours. Not every domain module is offered every term; the mix follows the research frontier.

Part III · 4h

自主发现平台实践与科研伦理

Platform practice and research ethics

把课堂学习转成可复现的科研实践，并守住责任边界。

Turn classroom learning into reproducible practice, with a clear responsibility boundary.

3.1 自主科学发现平台探索实践

3.1 Hands-on practice on discovery platforms

2h

邀请书生·端砚科学发现平台（上海人工智能实验室）等介绍工具链；学生分组选题，完成数据准备、建模计算、假设验证，形成可展示、可复现的实践作品。

Teams from platforms such as ShuSheng DuanYan (Shanghai AI Laboratory) introduce the toolchain. Students work in groups through data preparation, modeling, and hypothesis validation, and produce a shareable, reproducible artifact.

3.2 科学智能研究中的科研诚信与科学伦理

3.2 Research integrity and scientific ethics in AI4S

2h

诚信规范与责任边界；数据、模型、实验与成果的真实性、可追溯性与可复现性；幻觉、虚假引用、伪造与知识产权；生命健康、AI 安全、双重用途与负责任创新。

Integrity norms and responsibility boundaries; authenticity, provenance, and reproducibility; hallucination, fabricated citations, fraud, and intellectual property; biosafety, AI safety, dual use, and responsible innovation.

  • Agent Skills and how they apply to research
  • Turning high-frequency research steps into personal Skills
  • Finding, reusing, and refining strong research Skills

For researchers who want less repetitive work, clearer personal methods, and higher efficiency. No prior AI or programming background required.
